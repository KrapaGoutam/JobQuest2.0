## 2. Distinction Between Technical Recommendation & Production Authorization

### 2.1 Governance Policy Statement
During Milestone 14, operational recommendations were documented regarding plan tiers (OQ-016), smoke accounts (OQ-021), signing key custody (OQ-029), and rate limiting (OQ-030). These were marked as "RESOLVED" from an architectural and engineering feasibility standpoint.

**Crucial Clarification**:
- An engineering resolution indicates that the design, schema, and tooling are proven capable of supporting that configuration without technical defect.
- An engineering resolution **DOES NOT** grant authority to purchase cloud services, provision billable resources, connect to production databases, or redirect user traffic.
- Every production action remains in state **`PROPOSED — USER APPROVAL REQUIRED`** until the user explicitly answers the decisions in Section 1.
